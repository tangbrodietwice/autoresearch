# Scientific research

Read relevant prior trials before choosing a candidate. Cite the trial IDs used and explain the information gained.
State a falsifiable hypothesis, the expected observation and what would disconfirm it.
Change only the permitted experimental variable, preserving the baseline and evaluation protocol.
After a result, separate observed metrics from mechanism hypotheses. Consider alternatives and check guardrails.
A large or unexpected gain calls for verification of the evaluation and data before a stronger conclusion.
Keep negative and inconclusive results useful by recording a follow-up and its reason.
